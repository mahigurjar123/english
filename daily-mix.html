60-Din Mix Plan

Har din thoda-thoda — sab topics ek saath

Ek deep topic ki jagah, roz ek chhota mix milega. Din 1-30 = Foundations Mix — foundations, parts of speech, tense/modal, active-passive/narration, conversation aur mistakes. Din 31-60 = Advanced + Writing — mixed conditionals, reported speech, inversion jaise advanced grammar, formal-email/essay jaisi writing practice, aur professional vocabulary. Agla din tabhi khulega jab aaj ka din complete karoge.
⚠️Progress save kahan hota hai: Aapka progress is browser aur is exact address (URL) mein save hota hai. Agar aap kabhi file seedha kholte ho aur kabhi doosre tareeke se, ya browser badalte ho, to progress reset ho sakta hai — hamesha ek hi tarike se site kholiye.
