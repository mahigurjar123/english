12 Tenses

Part 5 · Modal Verbs

Can, Could, May, Might, Should, Must...

Modals helping verbs hain jo ability, permission, possibility, advice ya obligation dikhate hain. Modal ke baad hamesha V1 (base form) aata hai।
Part 5 · Voice

Active Voice aur Passive Voice

Active: Subject khud kaam karta hai. Passive: Subject par kaam hota hai — focus action par, karne wale par nahi। Formula: Object + is/am/are/was/were/has been + V3 + by + Subject.
Part 5 · Narration

Direct aur Indirect Speech

Direct Speech: kisi ki baat exact quotation marks ke saath. Indirect Speech: usi baat ko apne shabdon mein, bina quotation ke — tense, pronoun aur time-words badalte hain।
